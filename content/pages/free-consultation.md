## Get a clear picture of your finances with one conversation

Most business owners don't call an accountant because they've got it all figured out. They call because something feels off, a number doesn't add up, tax season is looming, or they're simply tired of guessing. If that sounds familiar, a free consultation with Abramson & Company is a good place to start.

There's no sales pitch and no obligation. You'll sit down with someone who reviews your numbers, listens to what's actually keeping you up at night, and gives you a straight answer about what makes sense next. Whether you run a real estate brokerage in Westport or a retail shop in Fairfield County, this first conversation is about clarity, not commitment.

<!-- block: process-steps | variant: horizontal -->
## What to expect during your free consultation

### Schedule a time that works for you
Pick a date on our calendar or reach out directly. Calls typically run 30 to 45 minutes and can happen by phone or video, whichever is easier for your schedule.

### Talk with a CPA, not a call center
You'll speak with someone from our team who works directly with clients, not a scripted intake person. Expect real questions about your business or personal finances.

### Cover what matters most to you
Bookkeeping backlogs, payroll headaches, an upcoming tax deadline, a business decision you're not sure about, bring whatever's on your mind. We'll cover bookkeeping, tax, payroll, or advisory needs depending on what you need.

### Leave with a clear next step
No pressure, no jargon. You'll know whether our services fit, and if they do, what working together would actually look like.

## Support tailored to your industry

Generic advice doesn't help much when your business has its own quirks. We work with clients across a handful of industries where the accounting details actually matter.

### Real Estate
icon: Home

Commission timing, 1031 exchanges, property-level bookkeeping. We understand how real estate professionals get paid and taxed differently from a typical business owner.

### Professional Service Providers
icon: Briefcase

From law firms to consultants, service-based businesses need clean books and smart entity structuring to keep more of what they bill.

### Retail
icon: Building2

Inventory, seasonal cash flow, and payroll for hourly staff all require a bookkeeping rhythm that matches how retail actually operates.

### Equine
icon: ShieldCheck

### Influencers
icon: Globe

Brand deals, 1099 income from multiple platforms, and irregular payment schedules need a bookkeeping approach built for that pace.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Schedule a free consultation

**Q: How long does a free consultation with Abramson & Company take?**
