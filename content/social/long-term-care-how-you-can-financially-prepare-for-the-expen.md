## LinkedIn

A private room in a nursing home now runs well over $100,000 a year. Multiply that by the average multi-year stay, and families are looking at $250,000 to $350,000 in long-term care costs that most retirement plans never budgeted for.

For business owners and families in Fairfield County, where home health aide rates already run above the national average, waiting until a parent needs help bathing or managing medication means paying retail rates under pressure. Insurance purchased in your 50s or early 60s, dedicated savings earmarked specifically for care, and VA Aid and Attendance benefits for eligible veterans and spouses can work together instead of leaving one strategy to carry the whole risk.
