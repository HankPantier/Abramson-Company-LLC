## Find the right form for your needs

Most accounting firms bury client paperwork in old email threads or a folder nobody can find twice. We built this page so that doesn't happen here. Whether you need a new-client engagement letter, a tax organizer, or the link to upload last year's 1099s, it lives on one page instead of six inboxes.

If you're a Westport, CT client, or you work with us from Fairfield County, the tri-state area, or the Bay Area, everything below points to the same secure system our team uses every day. No searching, no waiting on hold for a document you already know exists.

## Client onboarding and engagement documents

Starting with a new CPA firm usually means a stack of paperwork before anyone answers a real question. We keep the list short and specific:

- Engagement letter outlining scope, fees, and what to expect from bookkeeping, payroll, tax, or advisory work
- New client intake questionnaire covering entity type, prior-year returns, and current bookkeeping software
- Document checklist matched to your situation, whether that's a Schedule C, an S-corp, or a rental property
- Authorization forms for IRS or state correspondence, when your engagement calls for it

Each of these arrives through your secure portal login once we've scheduled your first meeting, so you're never filling out the same information twice.

## Secure document upload and client portal access

Email was never built to carry a W-2 or a bank statement safely, so we don't use it for that. Every client gets a login to our secure online platform, where you can upload documents, review drafts, and message your CPA in real time instead of waiting for a callback.

The portal is also where engagement letters, signed authorizations, and finished tax returns land once they're ready. If you've misplaced your login, a quick call or email to your contact person gets it reset the same day. This is the same system our team uses internally, so what you upload shows up on our end instantly, not after someone forwards an attachment three times.

## Payroll and bookkeeping resource links

Clients running payroll or managing books across Connecticut, New York, and New Jersey need quick access to the agencies that set the rules. Here are the links we send most often:

- Connecticut Department of Labor, for state unemployment and new-hire reporting
- IRS, for federal withholding tables and EIN applications
- New York State Department of Labor, for tri-state employees on payroll
- New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, for NJ-based staff
- Connecticut Department of Revenue Services, for state tax registration and filing

If a form on one of these sites doesn't match your situation, that's usually a sign it's time to loop in your bookkeeping or payroll contact here before you file anything.
