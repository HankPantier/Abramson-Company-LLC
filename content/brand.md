# About Abramson & Company LLC

## Identity

Abramson & Company LLC is a CPA firm headquartered at 278 Post Road East in Westport, Connecticut. Ray Abramson, CPA, founded the firm in its current form in 2005, and it operates under the tagline "We are passionate to meet your personal and business needs."

The team includes Ray Abramson, CPA (Principal), Kristen Rossi (Director of Operations), Lisa McEwan (Senior Accountant), Deborah Wamser, Chris Finelli, CPA, Rebecca Reineke, and Jason Smith Jr. (Accountants), Lindsay Brachle (Solution Specialist), and Beth Antunez (Admin). The firm describes its culture as team-oriented, with multiple team members familiar enough with each client to step in for one another, ensuring continuity of service.

The firm's tone is warm, reassuring, and client-focused, favoring plain-spoken, accessible language over technical jargon — reflecting a broader goal of reducing client stress and providing personalized support.

## Positioning & Differentiation

Abramson & Company positions itself as a CPA firm that treats every client like a neighbor, combining CPA-led expertise with genuine personal relationships and deep, specialized industry knowledge. This applies across real estate, professional services, retail, and nonprofit sectors, and extends to clients in Fairfield County, the tri-state area, the Bay Area, and nationwide.

The firm's stated differentiators include:

- Bundled services — bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory — for a fixed monthly fee
- A secure online platform for real-time collaboration with clients
- Personalized tax strategies rather than one-size-fits-all preparation
- CPA-led expertise spanning bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and business advisory

The firm identifies a core client need: people have access to a lot of financial information but lack someone they can talk to and trust for personal guidance. Abramson positions its role as filling that gap — acting less like a transactional preparer and more like an ongoing financial partner that helps clients protect income, minimize taxes, and grow with confidence.

Services offered include Bookkeeping, Outsourced Accounting (bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and audit protection with account reconciliation and quarterly KPI review), Tax Plus (year-round personal tax support including deduction analysis, tax projections, options analysis, and expatriate tax), Payroll, Tax, Business Foundation Services (entity analysis, accounting system setup, retirement plan analysis), Audit Protection (IRS notice handling, state/federal representation, appeals and litigation support), Business Management Services (budgeting, revenue/expense/COGS analysis, tax planning), Financial Advice and Planning, and Advisory.

## Industries Served

### Real Estate

Abramson & Company provides specialized accounting expertise for real estate professionals, one of the firm's core niches alongside professional services, retail, and nonprofits.

### Professional Service Providers

The firm supports professional service businesses, including small doctor offices, independent physician groups, attorneys, marketing companies, and video production companies. Services are tailored to the accounting and advisory needs specific to these practice types.

### Retail

Abramson & Company offers expert accounting and advisory support for the retail industry, applying its broader bundled-service model — bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory — to retail-specific needs.

### Equine

The firm serves clients with horse-related businesses and ownership, providing accounting support tailored to the equine industry. This is a quietly served niche rather than an actively promoted specialty.

### Influencers

Abramson & Company provides tax and accounting support for social media influencers. As with the equine niche, this is served quietly rather than featured as a primary marketing focus.
