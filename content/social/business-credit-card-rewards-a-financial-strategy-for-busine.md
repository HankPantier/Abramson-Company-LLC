## LinkedIn

A Westport retailer ran $340,000 through one business credit card last year, almost all inventory and shipping. A real estate agent nearby put through just $18,000, mostly gas and client dinners. Same question from both: is this card actually working for me?

Here's the detail most owners miss: buy $10,000 in inventory and earn $200 in cash back, and the deduction is $9,800, not the full amount, because the rebate reduces your real cost. Miss that distinction and your books overstate revenue and understate deductions.

The fix starts with matching the card's bonus categories to actual spending, not the flashiest sign-up offer, then reconciling rewards activity in the same platform your bookkeeper is already watching. Our outsourced accounting clients get that reconciliation folded into one fixed monthly fee, so it never turns into a March surprise.
