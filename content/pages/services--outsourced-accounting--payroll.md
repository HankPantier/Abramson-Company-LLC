## Payroll that gives you back your time

Most small business owners lose the better part of a full workday every month to payroll. Calculating hours, chasing tax deposits, fixing a misclassified employee before the filing deadline: it adds up, and it pulls you away from the work that actually grows your business.

At Abramson & Company, payroll is not a task we hand off to a junior processor and hope for the best. It's run by the same CPA-led team that handles your bookkeeping, tax, and advisory work, so nothing falls through the cracks between departments. You get your time back, and you get it back with the peace of mind that a CPA is watching the details.

## What our payroll services include

You should know exactly what you're paying for. Here's what's covered when Abramson & Company runs your payroll:

- Processing of scheduled payrolls, weekly, biweekly, or monthly
- New employee setup and onboarding paperwork
- Salary changes, bonuses, and off-cycle payments
- Federal, state, and local payroll tax filings
- W-2 and 1099 processing at year-end
- Time tracking integration so hours flow straight into pay runs
- Direct deposit and pay stub delivery for every employee
- Ongoing compliance checks so filing deadlines never slip

Each of these is handled by a CPA-supervised team, not a call center reading from a script. If a filing deadline is approaching or a tax rule changes, you hear about it before it becomes a problem.

Abramson & Company bundles payroll with the rest of your accounting support, bookkeeping, tax, and advisory, for one fixed monthly fee. ## Payroll built for your industry

Payroll doesn't look the same across industries, and treating it like a one-size template is where most providers get it wrong.

### Real Estate Teams
icon: Home

Agents often mix W-2 staff with 1099 contractors and commission-based pay. We structure payroll so commission splits, referral fees, and staff wages are classified correctly from the start, not corrected after an IRS notice.

### Professional Service Providers
icon: Briefcase

Law firms, consultancies, and agencies often carry bonus structures and partner draws alongside salaried staff. We keep those distinctions clean on every pay run and every tax filing.

### Retail Businesses
icon: Clock

Hourly staff, seasonal hiring, and shifting schedules make retail payroll a moving target. Time tracking integration and fast turnaround keep pay runs accurate even when your headcount changes month to month.

### Equine Operations
icon: Users

Barn staff, trainers, and seasonal help often span multiple pay structures and, sometimes, multiple states. We handle the classification and filing details so you can stay focused on the horses.

### Influencer Businesses
icon: Zap

Income that arrives in bursts, from brand deals to platform payouts, still needs consistent payroll and tax handling if you employ assistants or production help. We keep the paperwork steady even when the income isn't.
