## LinkedIn

A $150,000-income household with $20,000 in unreimbursed medical costs can deduct $8,750 of that, once expenses clear the 7.5% AGI threshold under IRS rules. Most people never track toward that number because the emergency fund conversation stops at cash reserves.

An HSA changes the math further: $8,300 in pretax contributions for a family HDHP plan in 2024, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals for qualified expenses. Real estate professionals and retail owners with uneven income streams need that kind of tax advantage as much as they need a savings cushion.
