## Accounting built around how video production companies work

Video production income rarely lands the same way twice. One month you're invoicing a client for a six-figure commercial shoot; the next you're waiting forty-five days on a production payment while covering gear rentals, freelance editors, and a small crew of contractors. Generic bookkeeping software wasn't built for that rhythm, and neither was the accountant down the street.

Abramson & Company works with production companies across Westport, CT and Fairfield County who need a CPA firm that already understands project-based revenue, equipment-heavy budgets, and a workforce that shifts from job to job. Our bookkeeping, payroll, and tax services are built around that reality instead of asking you to fit a standard small-business template. That means fewer surprises at tax time and a clearer read on cash flow between projects.

## Bookkeeping and outsourced accounting for production budgets and client billing

Production budgets rarely stay still. A single project might involve a client retainer, rental deposits, freelancer invoices, and reimbursable expenses, all moving at different speeds. Our [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping) and [outsourced accounting](/services/outsourced-accounting) services keep those pieces reconciled in one place, on a secure online platform where you see real-time numbers instead of waiting for a monthly PDF.

Bundled under one fixed monthly fee, this service covers:

- Project-by-project budget tracking so you know margin on each shoot, not just overall revenue
- Client invoicing and collections support to keep cash flow steady between productions
- Vendor and equipment rental payment tracking, so nothing slips through the cracks
- Monthly reconciliation and reporting your team can review without decoding spreadsheets

The goal is straightforward: your creative team spends time on production, not chasing receipts.

## Payroll for crews, freelancers, and contractors

A single shoot day might involve a full-time production manager on payroll, three freelance camera operators paid 1099, and a per-project gaffer working under a union agreement. Classifying each person correctly, and paying them on time, is where a lot of production companies get into trouble with the IRS or state labor departments.

Abramson & Company's [payroll](/services/payroll) service handles W-2 staff, 1099 contractor payments, and per-project crew in one system, so you're not juggling three separate processes for three types of workers. We track worker classification, filing deadlines, and year-end 1099 and W-2 issuance, and flag misclassification risk before it becomes a problem. Crews get paid accurately and on schedule, and you get a payroll record that holds up if a filing ever gets questioned.

## Tax planning and deductions specific to video and media production

Production companies carry deductions most small businesses never touch, and miss others because nobody flagged them. [Personalized tax strategy](/services/tax) for a media business looks different than for a retail shop, and that's where planning ahead of the return matters more than the return itself.

### Equipment depreciation
icon: Wallet

Cameras, lenses, lighting rigs, and edit bay hardware depreciate on a schedule that rewards planning your purchases, not just recording them after the fact. We help you time equipment buys against Section 179 and bonus depreciation rules so a big gear purchase works for your tax bill instead of against it.

### Home studio and office deductions
icon: Home

Many production owners run pre-production, editing, or client meetings from a home office or converted studio space. We calculate the deduction correctly, square footage, utilities, and depreciation included, so you claim what you're entitled to without raising a flag.

### Production-related expense deductions
icon: FileText

Location fees, travel to shoots, insurance, and post-production software subscriptions all carry deduction rules specific to project-based creative work. We track these against your bookkeeping records so nothing gets missed at filing time.

### Connecticut digital media tax credit guidance
icon: Award

Connecticut offers a sliding-scale tax credit for qualified digital media and motion picture production expenses. We help determine whether your production activity and spending qualify, and walk through the documentation the state requires.

## Business advisory support as your production company grows

Plenty of production companies start as one person with a camera and a laptop, then grow into an agency with a full crew, a studio lease, and multiple concurrent projects. Each stage raises a different question: stay a sole proprietor or move to an S-corp? Set up a formal budget process before hiring? How much to set aside for slow months?

[Business Foundation Services](/services/business-foundation-services/) help you choose the right entity structure and set up financial systems that scale with you, from a freelance operation to a full production house. [Business Management Services](/services/business-management-services) cover the longer view: profit distribution, retirement planning, and the financial decisions that come with owning a growing creative business.
