## Learn accounting and tax topics at your own pace

Questions about taxes, payroll, or bookkeeping don't wait for business hours, and sometimes you just want a straight answer before picking up the phone. Abramson & Company's video library gives Westport, CT clients, and visitors across Fairfield County and the tri-state area, a free way to get plain-spoken explanations on the topics that come up again and again: deductions, deadlines, payroll setup, and more. Each video is short and built around real questions we hear from small business owners, real estate professionals, and individuals filing their own returns. Watch what applies to you, skip what doesn't, and come back whenever a new question surfaces. If something you see raises a question specific to your situation, that's exactly what our team is here for.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Video library

**Q: What topics does the video library cover?**
A: The library covers personal and business tax planning, bookkeeping fundamentals, payroll setup and compliance, and niche guidance for real estate, retail, professional service, equine, and influencer clients. Videos are recorded by our CPA-led team in Westport, CT.

**Q: Is the video library free to watch?**
A: Yes. Every video is free to watch with no login required. The library was built so Westport, CT and tri-state area clients could get quick, plain-spoken answers to common tax, bookkeeping, and payroll questions without waiting for a call.

**Q: Are the videos specific to Connecticut tax rules?**
A: Many videos address federal rules that apply everywhere, but several cover state-specific topics relevant to clients across Connecticut, New York, and New Jersey, since we serve businesses and individuals throughout the tri-state area.

**Q: Can I get personalized advice after watching a video?**
A: Yes. The videos answer common questions, but every situation is different. You can schedule a consultation with our Westport, CT team to talk through what a video didn't cover for your specific tax, payroll, or bookkeeping needs.
