## Practical tools to make tax and accounting less stressful

Most people don't call their CPA until something's wrong: a letter from the IRS, a missed deadline, a payroll question nobody on staff can answer. The resource library at Abramson & Company gives you a place to go before that happens. It's a working collection of tools, videos, and guides built from questions clients in Westport, CT and across Fairfield County actually ask us.

You won't find generic checklists written for no one in particular. Every tool here connects to something our CPAs cover in real client conversations, whether that's tracking a refund, understanding a bookkeeping report, or figuring out what changes when you switch firms. Use what's useful, skip what isn't, and call us when you want to talk through the rest.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: refund-tracker-tool.jpg | alt: "Person checking tax refund status on a laptop at home" | query: "person checking laptop tax documents" -->
## Track your refund without the guesswork

Once a return is filed, the waiting starts, and that's usually when clients start emailing us asking where things stand. Our [refund tracker](/resources/refund-tracker) gives individual taxpayers a direct way to check status without playing phone tag with the IRS or guessing based on a website that updates once a day.

It's a small tool, but it fits a bigger point: our work with a client doesn't end the moment a return gets submitted. Filing day is one step in a relationship that runs all year, through estimated payments, life changes, and next year's planning.

## Watch and learn at your own pace

Not every question needs a meeting. The [video library](/resources/video-library) covers the topics that come up most often between appointments: how to read a bookkeeping report, what quarterly estimated taxes actually cover, when a Schedule C makes sense versus an S-corp election, and similar ground-level basics.

Think of it as the conversation you'd have with your CPA if you called with a quick question, minus the wait for a callback. Watch a five-minute video before your next meeting and you'll walk in already asking the right follow-up questions, which tends to make the meeting itself shorter and more useful.

## Guides built for your industry

A restaurant's bookkeeping questions look nothing like a real estate agent's, and a general checklist misses both. These industry resources reflect the niches Abramson & Company works in day to day, not a one-size list adapted after the fact.

### Professional Service Providers

Lawyers, consultants, and other billable-hour businesses face their own mix of quarterly tax planning and cash flow timing. Our [professional services guides](/industries/professional-service-providers) address both directly.

### Retail
icon: Receipt

Inventory accounting, sales tax across states, and seasonal cash flow swings shape how retail businesses need to plan. Browse [retail-focused resources](/industries/retail) for practical, sector-specific answers.

### Equine

## Answers to common questions clients ask

A few questions come up in almost every first meeting. Here's where clients usually start, and what tends to reassure them once they hear it out loud:

- **I don't have all my documents organized.** Bring what you have. Our team sorts out what's missing and tells you exactly what to gather next.
- **I'm switching from another accountant.** We request prior-year returns and files directly, so you're not stuck chasing down old paperwork yourself.
- **I'm not sure what deadlines apply to my business.** Between bookkeeping, payroll, and tax filings, deadlines shift by entity type. We map yours out at the start of the engagement.
- **I don't know what a flat monthly fee actually covers.** Bundled pricing under our Tax Plus and outsourced accounting services spells out exactly what's included, so nothing shows up as a surprise line item later.

If your question isn't here, that's what the next section is for.
