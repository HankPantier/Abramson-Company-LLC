Agents chasing commissions, brokers running teams, investors juggling five or six properties, developers mid-construction, property managers reconciling rent rolls: real estate income never looks the same from one client to the next. Most of what's online about real estate taxes is generic. It tells you depreciation exists but not how to apply it to your triplex in Westport or your flip in New Canaan.

What real estate professionals in Fairfield County actually need is someone who picks up the phone, knows the deal structure, and has already thought through the tax angle before the closing date arrives. Abramson & Company has worked with real estate clients across Westport, CT, Fairfield County, and the tri-state area since 2005, and we build the financial side of your business around how you actually earn money, not a template.

## Services tailored to property income and transactions

Property income rarely fits into one bucket. Rental cash flow, commission checks, 1031 proceeds, and construction draws each carry different tax treatment and different bookkeeping needs. Here's how our services line up against the real estate specifics that actually matter.

### Bookkeeping
icon: Calculator

We track rent rolls, security deposits, commission splits, and property-level expenses so every unit or listing has its own clean set of books, not one blended ledger that hides what's actually profitable.

### Outsourced Accounting
icon: Briefcase

For investors and developers managing multiple entities, we handle the full accounting function, month-end close, financial statements, and reconciliations across each LLC, so you get one clear picture instead of five disconnected spreadsheets.

### Tax Plus
icon: FileCheck

Ongoing tax support built around real estate timing: 1031 exchange deadlines, cost segregation studies, and depreciation schedules reviewed before decisions get made, not after the return is filed.

### Payroll
icon: Users

Property management teams and brokerages with support staff get payroll that handles commission-based pay structures and multi-state employees without the filing headaches.

### Tax
icon: Coins

Personal and business returns prepared with attention to passive activity rules, rental loss limitations, and the entity structure behind each property you hold.

### Business Foundation Services
icon: Building2

Setting up a new LLC for an acquisition or restructuring how you hold multiple properties? We help you choose an entity structure that fits your tax picture and your exit plan.

## Tax strategies for investors, agents, and property owners

Depreciation sounds simple until you're weighing a cost segregation study against the cost of the study itself, or deciding whether a rental loss is deductible this year under the passive activity rules. Agents face a different problem: commission income that swings wildly month to month, with quarterly estimates due whether the deal closed or not.

We build tax strategy around your calendar, not April's. That means reviewing depreciation schedules and entity structure before year-end, flagging when a cost segregation study makes sense on a new acquisition, and setting quarterly estimates that reflect what you're actually earning rather than last year's numbers. For clients holding several properties, we look at the portfolio as a whole so losses on one property aren't quietly wasted while another owes tax it didn't need to.

The goal is simple: fewer surprises at filing time, and a strategy you understood months before it mattered.
