## LinkedIn

A Westport business owner pulled three months of statements before a meeting with us and found $340 a month disappearing into fourteen subscriptions. Four, she couldn't identify at all.

That's subscription creep, and it typically costs households and small businesses $2,000 to $7,000 a year in charges nobody reviews. A retail shop layering POS add-ons. A video production company running fourteen SaaS tools it forgot it bought two years ago. Same pattern, bigger dollar signs.

The fix takes an hour: pull three months of statements, flag every recurring charge, cancel what's unused, and put what's left on a quarterly review instead of letting it run in the background. Clean bookkeeping catches these charges as they happen instead of eighteen months later.
