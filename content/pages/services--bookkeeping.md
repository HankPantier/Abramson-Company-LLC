## Bookkeeping support that gives you peace of mind

Falling behind on your books creates a quiet kind of stress. You know it's there even when you're not looking at it. For small business owners in Westport and across Fairfield County, that unease usually comes from not knowing where the numbers actually stand.

Abramson & Company built its bookkeeping service around a simple idea: you should never have to guess. Every transaction gets categorized, every account gets reconciled, and every report reaches you before you have to ask for it. Founded in 2005, our firm treats bookkeeping as the foundation everything else rests on, from tax planning to the advice you need to grow. When the books are clean, the rest of the picture gets a lot easier to see.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: bookkeeper-reviewing-documents.jpg | alt: "Bookkeeper reviewing financial documents and receipts at a desk" | query: "bookkeeper reviewing financial documents desk" -->
## What our bookkeeping services include

Bookkeeping done right is more than data entry. Our team categorizes every transaction, reconciles bank and credit card accounts monthly, and delivers financial statements you can actually read and use. Here's what's included:

- Transaction categorization and coding, done consistently every month
- Bank and credit card reconciliations to catch errors before they compound
- Monthly profit and loss statements and balance sheets
- Accounts payable and receivable tracking
- Custom reporting built around the metrics that matter to your business
- Direct coordination with your CPA on tax questions as they come up

Bookkeeping is also the entry point into two of our other services. Clients who want a fully outsourced finance function often move into [Outsourced Accounting](/services/outsourced-accounting), where we handle month-end close and management reporting. Those who want tax strategy woven into their monthly numbers add [Tax Plus](/services/tax), so nothing gets discovered for the first time in April.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: fixed-fee-consultation.jpg | alt: "Accountant explaining fixed monthly pricing to a small business owner" | query: "accountant discussing pricing with client" -->
## One fixed monthly fee, no surprises

Hourly billing punishes you for asking questions. Call your bookkeeper twice in a week and you might see it reflected on the invoice. We don't work that way.

Every bookkeeping client pays one fixed monthly fee that bundles bookkeeping with the related services you need, whether that's payroll, tax prep, or advisory support. You know the number before the month starts, and it doesn't move because you emailed us three times instead of one.

That predictability matters most when cash flow is already tight. Fixed-fee bundling lets you budget for accounting the way you budget for rent or payroll, without worrying that a busy month turns into a surprise bill.

## Real-time access through our secure client platform

Not knowing where your numbers stand between meetings is its own kind of stress. Our secure online platform gives you real-time access to your books, so you can check your cash position at 9pm on a Tuesday without waiting for a callback.

You'll see reconciled transactions, current financial statements, and shared documents in one place. If a question comes up, you can flag it directly in the platform instead of digging through an email thread from three weeks ago.

It's the same information we're looking at, updated on the same schedule we update it, so you're never working from an outdated picture of your own business.

## Bookkeeping tailored to your industry

Every industry keeps its books a little differently. A property manager's chart of accounts doesn't look like a boutique's, and a veterinary practice tracks costs a retail shop never has to think about. We've built bookkeeping workflows around the niches we know best, plus a few industries most local firms overlook.

<!-- block: industry-cards | variant: 3-col | theme: ink -->
### Real Estate
icon: Home

Property managers and investors need bookkeeping that tracks income and expenses by property, not just by month. We reconcile security deposits, CAM charges, and escrow so reports match what your lender expects to see. See our [real estate accounting work](/industries/real-estate).

### Professional Service Providers
icon: Briefcase

Law firms, consultants, and agencies bill by project or retainer, and your books should reflect that. We track work-in-progress and unbilled time so revenue recognition lines up with cash actually collected. Learn more about our [professional service provider support](/industries/professional-service-providers).

### Retail
icon: Building2

Inventory, point-of-sale integration, and sales tax across jurisdictions complicate retail books fast. We reconcile daily sales data against deposits so shrinkage and errors surface quickly. Explore our [retail bookkeeping approach](/industries/retail).

### Equine
icon: HeartPulse

Boarding fees, veterinary costs, and seasonal cash flow make equine businesses harder to track than most bookkeepers realize. We've built the categories that fit how horse operations actually run.

### Influencers
icon: Sparkles

Brand deals, affiliate income, and gifted product all count as revenue, and the IRS expects it reported correctly. We track irregular income streams so tax season doesn't turn into a scramble.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing, retainage, and progress billing require bookkeeping that goes deeper than a standard general ledger. We track costs by job so you know which projects actually make money.

### Healthcare
icon: Stethoscope

Insurance reimbursements, payer mix, and practice overhead move on their own timeline. We reconcile receivables against what payers actually remit, not just what was billed.

We built a team-oriented culture on purpose. More than one person on our staff knows your account well enough to answer a question if your usual contact is out or on vacation. You're never stuck waiting for one specific person to call back.

For clients across Westport, Fairfield County, and the tri-state area, that continuity means fewer dropped balls and fewer repeated conversations. You explain your business once, and the whole team carries that context forward.
