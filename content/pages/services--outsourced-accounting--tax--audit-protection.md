## Fear no audit with a CPA-led defense team

An IRS notice in the mailbox can ruin a week. Most business owners open it, feel their stomach drop, and have no idea what happens next. That's the moment Abramson & Company steps in.

We respond to the IRS and Connecticut Department of Revenue Services directly on your behalf, so you're not the one on the phone trying to decode agency language. Since founding the firm in 2005, Ray Abramson has built a team that treats audit defense as a core discipline, not an afterthought bolted onto tax prep. You get a CPA between you and the auditor, handling correspondence, deadlines, and documentation while you keep running your business. That's the whole point of audit protection: fewer sleepless nights, one clear point of contact, and someone in your corner who already knows your file.

## What our audit protection covers

Audit protection through Abramson & Company isn't a single phone call. It's a defined scope of work that stays active for as long as an inquiry runs, covering:

- Direct response to IRS notices and letters, including CP2000s, underreporter notices, and audit selection letters
- Handling of Connecticut Department of Revenue Services notices and other state tax authority correspondence
- Representation at the examination and appeals level, so you're not sitting across the table alone
- Support through litigation-track matters when a case escalates beyond standard appeals
- Ongoing communication with the assigned agent or auditor, tracked and documented so nothing falls through a gap
- Coordination with your bookkeeping and tax records already on file with our team, cutting down the scramble to find documentation

Every response goes out under CPA review before it reaches the IRS or state. You'll know what's been sent, when, and why.

## Audit protection tailored to your industry

Auditors look for different red flags depending on what you do. Generic advice misses the details that actually trigger a notice, so our approach shifts by industry.

### Real Estate
icon: Home

Depreciation schedules, 1031 exchanges, and passive activity loss rules draw scrutiny in real estate audits. We prepare documentation on entity structure and property transactions before an inquiry ever arrives.

### Professional Service Providers
icon: Briefcase

Attorneys, consultants, and other service providers face questions about business use of home offices, owner compensation, and deductible expenses. We keep the paper trail organized so those answers are ready.

### Video Production Companies
icon: Video

Production costs, equipment depreciation, contractor payments, and mixed personal-business expenses are common audit triggers for video production companies. We document your project expenses and contractor classifications so the records hold up under scrutiny.

### Retail
icon: Building2

Inventory valuation, cash transactions, and sales tax reconciliation are common audit triggers for retail businesses. We tie your point-of-sale and bookkeeping records together so the numbers match every filing.

## What to expect if you receive an audit notice

### Call us before you respond to anything

Don't sign, mail, or email anything back to the IRS or state on your own. Call your Abramson & Company contact first, ideally within 24 to 48 hours of opening the notice.

### Send us the notice

Upload the letter through the secure client portal or forward it directly. We confirm receipt and flag the response deadline immediately so nothing lapses.

### We review and respond

A CPA on your account reviews the notice against your existing records, drafts the response, and handles direct correspondence with the agency on your behalf.

### We keep you posted

You get a plain-language update at each stage, whether the matter closes in one letter or moves toward an appeals-level review.
