## LinkedIn

A guard dog patrolling your warehouse or retail shop overnight can be a real business expense under IRS Publication 535. A family pet sleeping in your home office, no matter how well-behaved, isn't.

We work with retail owners, property managers, and video production companies across Westport and Fairfield County who ask this exact question, especially when the dog also plays a role in monetized content or on-site security. The IRS wants proof: training records, hours logged on-site, or documentation tying the animal to income or a diagnosed medical need. Service and guide dogs fall under Publication 502, but only the portion of medical costs above 7.5% of your adjusted gross income counts, meaning a household with $150,000 in AGI needs $11,250 in expenses before any of it helps.

If you're not sure which side of the line your situation lands on, our latest post breaks down guard dogs, service animals, breeding operations, and influencer pets one at a time: https://www.abramsonco.com/resources/unless-its-working-for-you-your-dog-isnt-a-tax-deduction
