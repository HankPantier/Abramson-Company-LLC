## LinkedIn

14 days. That's the line the IRS uses to decide whether your second home is a tax-free perk or a rental business.

Rent it 14 days or fewer in a year and the income is tax-free: no Schedule E, no reporting, no cap on what you charge. Cross into 15 rental days, and personal-use days take over. Use the property more than 14 days or 10% of rental days, and expenses are deductible only up to rental income. Stay under that threshold, and losses may be deductible under the passive activity rules.

For a client with a Litchfield Hills property or a Vermont condo, that distinction is worth real money, and it can flip from one year to the next depending on how many weekends you actually use.

Read how the days add up: https://www.abramsonco.com/resources/how-to-turn-a-second-home-into-a-tax-win
