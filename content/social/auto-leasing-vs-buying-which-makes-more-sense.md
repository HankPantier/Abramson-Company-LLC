## LinkedIn

A heavy-duty truck kept five-plus years can qualify for a Section 179 deduction of up to $30,500 in 2024, on top of standard depreciation. A leased sedan replaced every two years can't touch that number, but it often wins on cash flow and simplicity instead.

That's the real question for real estate agents logging 20,000 miles a year or video production owners hauling gear between shoots: not what the monthly payment looks like, but how the vehicle is titled, how long you keep it, and what your entity structure allows.

Our new post breaks down the Section 179 math, the lease inclusion adjustment, and the mileage overage charges (15 to 30 cents per mile) that can turn a good lease deal into an expensive one at turn-in.
