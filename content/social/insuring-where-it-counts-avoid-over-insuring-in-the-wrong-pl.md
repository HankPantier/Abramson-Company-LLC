## LinkedIn

A Westport family holds a $300,000 umbrella policy on a $2.5 million home. Nearby, a business owner generating $400,000 a year in revenue carries no disability coverage at all. One of these is a minor inefficiency. The other is one bad diagnosis away from losing the business.

Insurance sizing isn't about buying every policy on a checklist. It's math: your net worth, your liquid savings, your dependents' income needs, weighed against what a worst-case loss would actually cost you. A $1 million term life policy often runs $600 to $1,200 a year, closing a real gap far more efficiently than years of extra saving ever could.

For real estate agents, retail owners, and professional service providers, this exposure changes yearly as revenue and assets shift. We walk clients through this same math during annual tax planning, not after something goes wrong.
