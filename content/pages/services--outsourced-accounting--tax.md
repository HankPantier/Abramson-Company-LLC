## Personalized business tax support for Westport and tri-state area businesses

Most tax prep feels transactional: drop off documents, wait, sign, done. Abramson & Company has worked differently since Ray founded the firm in 2005, treating every Westport, Fairfield County, and tri-state area business as more than just a folder of receipts. When a question comes up in March or in September, someone who already understands your return and your industry picks up the phone.

That matters because tax law changes every year, and generic advice rarely fits a specific business situation. A CPA looks at your return in the context of your whole financial picture—whether that's a portfolio of rental properties, a consulting practice with variable income, a video production project with complex expenses, or a retail operation navigating inventory and sales tax rules. The goal isn't just an accurate filing. It's a plan you understand and trust, built by people who stay in your corner after April 15.

## What our tax services cover

Tax touches every part of a client's finances, so Abramson & Company built its services to work together instead of in isolation.

### Tax Planning & Preparation
icon: Calculator

Federal, state, and local business returns for LLCs, S-corps, and partnerships, prepared with an eye toward next year's return, not just this one. Quarterly estimates, entity structure reviews, and industry-specific deduction strategy are built into the process rather than billed as an afterthought.

### Audit Protection
icon: ShieldCheck

Representation and support if the IRS or Connecticut Department of Revenue Services sends a letter, so you're not facing an inquiry alone or paying by the hour for peace of mind.

Because tax outcomes depend on clean books and accurate payroll records, these services connect directly to [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping) and [payroll](/services/payroll), giving your CPA a full, current picture of your business before decisions get made, not after.

## How our fixed monthly fee model simplifies tax planning

Most firms bill by the form, the hour, or the phone call, which makes tax costs unpredictable and turns every question into a line item.

- One flat monthly fee bundles tax planning, preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, and advisory support, so there's no surprise invoice for asking a question in October.
- A secure online platform lets you upload documents, message your CPA, and review your return in real time, from a phone, a laptop, or a client sitting in California at 9 a.m. Pacific.
- Personalized tax strategies get built around your actual return, not a generic checklist, because planning happens throughout the year instead of during a two-week rush in April.

This model exists because clients told Abramson & Company the same thing for years: they wanted to know what tax help would cost before they called, not after. For a deeper look at proactive planning, [see the firm's guide to tax refund strategy](/resources/e-books/maximizing-your-tax-refund-a-strategic-guide-to-tax-planning).

## A team that knows your tax situation, not just your file

No single person holds your entire tax file at Abramson & Company. Multiple team members review each client's return, income situation, and history closely enough to answer a question when your usual contact is out of office or on vacation.

That's a deliberate structure, not an accident. A team-oriented culture means calls get returned even during the first week of April, when solo practitioners often go dark. Continuity matters more during a tax question than almost any other part of running a business, because timing drives outcomes: a missed estimate deadline or an overlooked deduction can cost real money. Clients get a team that already knows their situation, not a single point of failure who happens to be reachable that day.
