## CPA expertise built around your industry

Westport and Fairfield County are home to more specialized businesses than most CPA firms give credit for: real estate brokers juggling closings, retail owners managing seasonal swings, video production companies tracking project-based income. Generic tax advice doesn't hold up against that kind of complexity, and neither does a bookkeeper who has never seen a commission statement from a closing or a production budget reconciliation.

Abramson & Company pairs CPA-led expertise with niche-specific knowledge, so clients get someone who already understands the shape of their business before the first meeting. That matters when the real problem isn't a lack of information, it's the lack of one person to call who actually knows the answer. Team members cross-train on each client file, so whoever picks up the phone knows the history, not just the account number.

## Real estate professionals

Agents, brokers, and investors deal with income that arrives in uneven bursts: a slow spring, a six-closing summer, a 1031 exchange that reshapes the whole tax picture. Abramson & Company builds tax strategies around that rhythm instead of forcing it into a standard quarterly estimate. Bookkeeping stays current on the secure online platform, so a broker can check commission income and expense categories from a phone between showings.

For real estate investors, that means tracking depreciation, entity structure, and capital gains exposure across multiple properties, not just filing a return once a year. Clients across Fairfield County and the wider tri-state area use this support to plan a purchase or sale months ahead, not the week before closing.

## Professional service providers

Law firms, consultants, and other service-based businesses often patch together a bookkeeper, a payroll vendor, and a tax preparer who never talk to each other. Abramson & Company bundles bookkeeping, payroll, and tax under one fixed monthly fee, so a small law practice or consulting firm gets one CPA-led team instead of three disconnected vendors.

That matters most for law firms, a segment many CPA firms overlook in favor of larger corporate accounts. Abramson & Company works directly with attorneys and consultants across Westport and the tri-state area on trust accounting questions, quarterly estimates, and the cash flow gaps that come with retainer-based billing.

## Retail businesses

Retail owners run on cash flow, and cash flow runs on timing: payroll due Friday, inventory due Monday, a holiday season that makes or breaks the year. Abramson & Company helps retail clients plan payroll and seasonal tax obligations before they become a scramble, using a secure online platform where owners and their bookkeeper see the same numbers in real time.

That real-time visibility means a shop owner in Westport or elsewhere in Fairfield County can check margins after a big sale weekend instead of waiting for a month-end report that's already out of date.

## Video production companies

Production budgets, project-based income, equipment depreciation, and crew payroll create an accounting mix that most general firms aren't built for. Abramson & Company works with video production companies on exactly this: tracking income and expenses by project, handling depreciation on cameras and gear, and keeping payroll organized across a crew that may change from job to job.

Bookkeeping runs through a secure online platform, so production owners can see where a project stands financially without waiting for a month-end report that arrives after the edit is already done.
