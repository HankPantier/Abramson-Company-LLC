## A CPA firm built on personal connection

Westport is a small town with a lot of financial complexity moving through it: family businesses, real estate transactions, retail storefronts, and professional practices that all need more than a once-a-year tax appointment. Abramson & Company was built around a simple idea: clients need someone to actually talk to, not another portal full of unread articles.

That's the gap we work to close. Small business owners and individuals in Fairfield County have access to more financial information than ever, but not enough of it comes with a person attached. A CPA firm should feel like a resource you can call, not a form you have to fill out. Every service we offer, from bookkeeping to business advisory work, is built to give clients a direct line to guidance they can trust.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: firm-founding-story-2005.jpg | alt: "Founder Ray reviewing financial documents at his Westport CPA office" | query: "cpa founder reviewing documents office" -->
## Our story since 2005

Ray founded Abramson & Company in its current form in 2005, and the firm has operated out of Westport, CT, ever since. Two decades in one town builds a certain kind of institutional memory: we've worked with clients through market downturns, business sales, generational transitions, and every tax law change in between.

Longevity matters when you're choosing someone to handle your finances. A firm that's been advising the same community since 2005 has already made its mistakes, refined its processes, and built the systems that let it respond quickly when a client calls with a problem. That history is part of what clients are buying when they sign on with us, not just a set of services but a firm that's still standing and still local.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: team-collaboration-client-file.jpg | alt: "Two CPA team members reviewing a client account together at a desk" | query: "accounting team collaborating office desk" -->
## A team that knows you, not just your file

One of the most common complaints about accounting firms is the single point of failure: your main contact goes on vacation, changes roles, or leaves, and suddenly nobody can answer a simple question. We built our team culture to avoid that problem entirely.

Multiple people on our staff know each client's business, not just their file number. If your usual contact is out, someone else can pick up the phone and already understand your entity structure, your payroll schedule, or where your tax return stands. It's a small operational choice with a big effect on how supported clients feel, especially during tax season or a business transition when timing matters most.

## Serving Westport, Fairfield County, and beyond

Our home base is Westport, CT, and most of our client relationships start close to home. But the firm's reach extends well past the town line, following clients as their businesses and lives grow.

- Westport, CT and the surrounding Fairfield County towns
- The tri-state area spanning Connecticut, New York, and New Jersey
- Clients in California, with a concentration in the Bay Area
- Remote collaboration for clients who split time between coasts

Geography rarely limits what a CPA relationship can accomplish once secure, real-time collaboration tools are in place, which is exactly how we work with clients outside Fairfield County.

## Our approach to accounting, tax, and advisory

How we work matters as much as what we do. Three things set the day-to-day experience apart from a typical CPA relationship.

### Bundled services, one fixed fee
icon: DollarSign

Bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory support are bundled under a fixed monthly fee, so clients know their cost before the year starts instead of guessing at an hourly bill.

### A secure platform for real-time collaboration
icon: ShieldCheck

Documents, financials, and messages move through a secure online platform, so clients can see where things stand and reach their team without waiting for a callback.

### Personalized tax strategies
icon: Target

Tax plans are built around each client's actual entity structure and income sources, not a generic checklist applied to every return.

### CPA-led expertise across every service
icon: BadgeCheck

Every engagement, from [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping) to [payroll](/services/payroll) to [business advisory work](/services/business-management-services), is overseen by CPA-led staff rather than handed off to junior processors.
