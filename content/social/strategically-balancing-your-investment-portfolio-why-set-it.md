## LinkedIn

A client's portfolio looked fine on paper: steady returns, familiar funds, nothing dramatic. But it hadn't been touched since 2019, and the equity allocation had drifted from 65% to nearly 82%. He was carrying far more risk than he realized, without ever deciding to take it on.

That's the quiet cost of set-it-and-forget-it investing. A 60/40 mix left alone for seven years can drift to 79% stocks, turning a moderate-risk plan into an aggressive one nobody chose. For Westport small business owners and real estate professionals, whose personal accounts often move in the same direction as their business income, that drift compounds fast.

Tax-aware rebalancing, reviewed alongside a CPA who knows both your investments and your tax return, keeps the plan tied to your actual goals instead of whatever the market did last.
